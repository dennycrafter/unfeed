# Good morning

Everything on the list is done, merged and on your phone. Nothing failed.

## What's fixed

1. **Your activity** no longer jumps to Messages. Single posts and reels always open now, on Instagram and LinkedIn. Home, the Reels tab and Explore are still blocked.
2. **LinkedIn Recent activity** shows posts again, on profiles and on the "Show all posts" page.
3. **Stories** never shows the home feed, not even for a moment. The app reads the story list in a hidden page and shows its own row of circles. Tap one to watch.
4. **Video sound** in DMs and stories: videos now start with sound. If you mute one yourself, the app leaves it alone.
5. **Settings gear** now looks like a gear.
6. **LinkedIn Profile** is at the bottom left.
7. **Blocked pages**: the bottom of Settings lists the last 20 pages the app stopped, with the time, the address and the rule. If something bounces, send me a screenshot of that list.

## What's new

- **Its own look.** Both apps now have the app's own top bar and bottom bar, in the same style. The sites' own bars and "get the app" banners are hidden.
- **Instagram tabs:** Stories, DMs, Search, Post, Profile.
- **LinkedIn tabs:** Profile, Messages, Search, Post, Notifications. Each tab remembers where you were.
- **Top bar:** Link (paste a link) on the left, plus QR on LinkedIn. Settings on the right.
- **Colours:** the pages are asked to use the app's dark colours and font. How well that works can only be seen on the phone.

## Test on your phone

1. Open Expo Go and open unfeed. If it still looks old, close it fully and open it again (it can take two tries to pick up the update).
2. Instagram: Profile, then Settings, then Your activity. Tap a liked post and a reel. Both should open, no jump to Messages.
3. Tap the **Stories** tab. You should see circles, never the feed. Tap one, watch, and let it finish. You should land back on the circles.
4. Open a DM with a video. It should play with sound. Mute it, then play another one: it should stay muted.
5. Look at the gear at the top right. It should look like Settings.
6. Switch to LinkedIn. The bottom bar should read Profile, Messages, Search, Post, Notifications.
7. LinkedIn: open someone's profile, scroll to Activity, tap "Show all posts". Posts should show.
8. LinkedIn: try Search (type a name), Notifications and Post (then close the post box).
9. Switch between tabs on both apps and check each one comes back where you left it.
10. Open Settings and scroll to the bottom. "Blocked pages" should be there.
11. Tell me if the new look is a keeper.
